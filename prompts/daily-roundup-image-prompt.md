# Daily Roundup Image Prompt

Create a wide 16:9 editorial hero image for The Disclosure Files daily UAP community roundup. Treat the day’s strongest documented theme as visual journalism, not fantasy: [INSERT THE DAY’S CENTRAL STORY OR DISCUSSION]. Use a dark midnight palette with archival paper texture, restrained teal signal marks, one amber or crimson editorial accent, and a cinematic but skeptical investigative mood. Show an ambiguous visual metaphor tied to the reporting—such as a distant light, radar trace, document folder, runway, witness location, or sensor display—without asserting an extraordinary explanation. No aliens, no cliché flying saucer, no sensational beams, no text, no logos, no invented evidence. Leave clean negative space for a headline overlay. The image should capture attention, summarize the emotional and factual center of the entry, and make readers want to read while preserving the distinction between documented fact, witness account, interpretation, and speculation.
